Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Zaiqa Habib Catering Social Media Manager is a content management tool designed to help prepare and manage social media content.

The service may process information that a user voluntarily provides, such as content, videos, captions, or other information needed to prepare social media posts.

Social Media Accounts

If a user connects a supported social media platform, the service may process information required to provide the requested social media management functionality. Users remain responsible for reviewing and approving content before publishing.

Content Publishing

Publishing actions require user confirmation before they are performed. The service is designed not to publish content without the user's explicit confirmation.

Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information processed by the service. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Third-Party Services

The service may interact with third-party social media platforms when users choose to connect them. Those platforms have their own privacy policies and terms of service.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, please contact Zaiqa Habib Catering through the contact information made available by the service.
